# OpenAI with talk

A natural voice that reads English words as a developer says them. The band's highlight is estimated from the length
of each word, close enough to follow along.

## Setting it up

1. Create a key on platform.openai.com.
2. Give it to the mod as the [README](../README.md#voices) says, then `/talk test` reads the sample sentence with it.

## What it costs

It is paid as used, with no plan: about a cent and a half per minute of voice. Beside the engine, the band shows an
estimate in dollars of what the session spent.

## Its voices

Its thirteen voices are listed in the band, to hear on the same sentence and choose. The mod asks the voice for a lively,
even pace, and the band's speeds go from half to twice the normal one.

The default model, `gpt-4o-mini-tts`, is announced to retire on 2027-01-06; the `OpenAI model` option names another.
